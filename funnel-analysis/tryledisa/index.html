📋 Executive Summary

Ledisa is a direct-to-consumer wellness patch brand running 15 active ads across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Threads as of May 2026. Their entire ad spend is concentrated on a single hero product — the GLP-1 Patch — positioned as a natural alternative to GLP-1 weight-loss drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy), targeting women struggling with food cravings and appetite control.

The brand is running two distinct funnels simultaneously:

  • Funnel 1 — tryledisa.com (MOFU/Product Page): 6 video ads driving to the main e-commerce homepage with a "Learn more" CTA. The landing page features a gamified scratch-card pop-up discount mechanic, social proof wall, full product range, and a patch-delivery education section.
  • Funnel 2 — quiz.patchesquiz.com/fb1-18p-email-quiz (TOFU/Quiz): 9 image ads driving to a live email-capture quiz. Capture the email, then nurture into the sale via email + retargeting.

Key copy insight: All 15 ads share nearly identical primary copy — a benefit-stacked formula using checkmark emojis, a money-back guarantee anchor, and a discount hook. The only variation: MOFU ads (product-page funnel) open with "Ledisa GLP-1 Patches are powered by..." (brand-led) vs. TOFU ads (quiz funnel) opening with "GLP-1 Patches are powered by..." (product-led). Creative is the sole testing variable.

🔑 Key Strategic Takeaways

1. GLP-1 trend surfing: Ledisa's entire strategy is built on the cultural awareness of GLP-1 drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy). Their product name and copy directly borrow the drug's language to capture intent-aware audiences without needing a prescription angle.

2. Copy is locked, creative is the variable: Every single ad uses the same ~80-word copy block. The testing lever is purely creative (video angle, image design, video length). This is a mature DTC testing framework — validate copy first, then scale by finding winning visuals.

3. Dual-funnel architecture with clear intent separation: MOFU (video → brand product page → explore) vs. TOFU (image → live email-capture quiz at quiz.patchesquiz.com/fb1-18p-email-quiz). The quiz captures emails and qualifies leads, then nurtures them into the sale via email + retargeting.

4. Triple urgency stacking in TOFU quiz ads: The quiz funnel ads layer three urgency signals: "50% OFF for a Limited Time," "[Low Stock Alert]," and "Mother's Day Sale / LAST CHANCE." Heavy urgency on a TOFU placement is unusual — typical TOFU is curiosity-led, not scarcity-led. Ledisa is using sale urgency to drive cold-traffic clicks INTO the quiz, where the email capture happens — then the real selling happens in the email sequence after.

5. Gamified pop-up on landing page: The tryledisa.com homepage opens with a scratch-card mechanic — a high-engagement format that captures email and delivers a discount before users even browse the products.

6. Female-targeted social proof: All named testimonials are from women. "Thousands of Women Trust Ledisa" is explicit gender targeting — the brand has a clearly defined buyer persona.

7. Patch delivery as the differentiator: The site devotes major real estate to educating users on why patches beat pills and gummies — key objection-handling for a novel format. The "Why Patches Work Better" section directly addresses category skepticism before it becomes a purchase barrier.
