The Strategy

🎯 The Offer & Positioning

  • 💊Product: GLP-1 patches — a natural, non-prescription alternative to Ozempic / Wegovy.
  • 👩Target buyer: Women dealing with food cravings, weight-loss frustration, and skepticism or cost barriers around the prescription drugs.
  • 🪝The hook: Ingredient credibility (Berberine, Prebiotics) wrapped in the GLP-1 vocabulary the audience already knows.
  • 🛡️Risk removal: 90-day money-back guarantee removes purchase risk; “natural” removes the drug stigma.

📝 The Copy Formula

  • 🔒One locked copy block, all 96 ads. Ingredient name-drop → 4 ✅ benefit bullets (cravings, fullness, metabolism, natural GLP-1) → 90-day guarantee → 50% OFF urgency close.
  • 🎛️Copy = control variable. Never reword the body. The team only varies the creative and the persona running the ad.
  • 🔀The only copy-level switches: brand-led opener (“Ledisa GLP-1 Patches…”) vs product-led opener (“GLP-1 Patches…”), and whether the headline carries the “[Low Stock Alert]” urgency tag.

👥 The Avatar Trinity — the actual scaling lever

The brand runs the same offer from 10 FB pages, in three persona buckets. Each archetype does a specific psychological job:

🏷️ Sister-brand pages
Same look-and-feel under different page names. Job: ad-account capacity and isolated audience pools. If one page hits a learning-phase wall or gets dinged by Meta, the others keep spending.
🩺 Doctor avatars
Whitelisted pages with medical-authority framing (Dr. prefix). Job: credibility signal for a health product on cold traffic. The Dr. prefix does most of the heavy lifting before anyone reads a word of copy.
👩 House-mom avatars
Personal-name pages. Job: peer-to-peer trust. The ad reads as “a regular woman who tried this” rather than “a brand selling to me.”

🔀 The Funnel Architecture

  • 🎯Funnel A — TOFU (70 ads): ads → quiz layer. Quiz captures email, qualifies the lead, then nurtures to sale via email + retargeting.
  • 🛍Funnel B — MOFU (26 ads): ads → product page. User is in active consideration, browsing the product range with a scratch-card discount mechanic on-site.
  • ✨One CTA everywhere: every ad says “Learn more.” The urgency stack in the headline is for click-rate, not for stage signaling.
  • 🔗Both routed via a trvl.<brand>.com UUID-pathed click-tracking layer (not rendered live in this snapshot).

🔑 What to Steal

  1. 1The avatar trinity. For any health/wellness product, run a doctor-persona page + a house-mom-persona page + your brand page concurrently. Doctor handles credibility, mom handles relatability, brand handles brand-building.
  2. 2Lock the copy. Vary the creative and the persona. Once a copy block works, treat it as a control variable. Use creative angles + persona as your test axes — vastly higher signal than rewording the same value props.
  3. 3Borrow the GLP-1 vocabulary. The category creates intent. Speak that vocabulary in the opener; deliver a natural-ingredient mechanism after the hook.
  4. 4Run two funnels in parallel. TOFU quiz (cheap lead capture) + MOFU product page (warm-clickers who skip the quiz). Different ad creative for each — image for the quiz, video for the product page.
  5. 5Reverse-search the landing domain. If you only watch a competitor’s primary FB page, you miss the shadow personas. Search the Ad Library by their landing-page domain to surface every page running ads to that funnel.
