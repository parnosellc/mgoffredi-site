The Strategy

🎯 The Offer & Positioning

  • 💊5+ SKUs across pet wellness: Hip & Joint (the flagship), Allergy & Immune, Anal Glands, Probiotics, Skin & Coat, Multivitamin.
  • 🐕Target buyer: Dog owners watching their dog suffer — joint stiffness, chronic itching, mobility decline, GI issues. High emotional load. Frustration with traditional vet care that "only masks symptoms."
  • 🎬100% video creative — no image ads in any capture. The brand's entire creative spend is on motion.
  • 🌍Apex domains: pawfy.com (global) and pawfy.co.uk (UK), plus campaigns.pawfy.com as a dedicated advertorial subdomain.

🩺 The Vet-Debunking Narrative — the brand's defining edge

Pawfy doesn't position against other supplements — they position against vets. The brand voice openly challenges the traditional veterinary approach to allergies/joints and frames their products as the root-cause fix vets won't or can't prescribe.

Bandaid analogy
"Vets Use Bandaids for Dog Allergies?" — "most medications are as useful as putting a band-aid on a broken leg."
Cost reframe
"Why is this $100 bill on fire? Vet dermatologist shares why your dog's allergies are so expensive and what actually works."
Gut as root cause
"What if I told you that more often than not it's because you're not dealing with allergies based on external factors… the gut houses up to 80% of your dog's immune system."
Belief reframe
"Everyone thinks it's grass or pollen… but what if it's not? 👀"

🔄 The Pixel-Seed Retargeting Play

  • 🎯13 of the ~28 ads have no CTA. They're not built to convert cold traffic — they're built to build the retargeting audience.
  • 📺Hook formats vary: direct question ("Do you want your dog to stop itching?"), empathy mirror ("You've probably already googled…"), curiosity ("What your dog is desperately trying to tell you…"), fear ("These supplements are ruining your dog's health"), and emotional pain ("The hardest part about being a dog parent isn't the vet bills…").
  • 🪤The play: seed interest with a no-ask video → Meta builds the custom audience → retarget with the direct-offer advertorial → close warm. Most brands skip step one and pay full CPM to convert cold; Pawfy splits the work.

📊 Copy Testing & The Scaled Winner

  • 🏆Single proven winner running 9×: "Over 20 million dogs struggle with allergies every day. But are we helping more than hurting? This video breaks it all down." 9 near-identical versions running simultaneously — the team's scaled bet.
  • 🎨Creative angles tested: 📊 Stat hook · ❓ Curiosity · 📝 Story opener · 🤝 Empathy mirror · ⚠️ Fear/agitation · 😬 Problem-aware · 🎯 Pattern interrupt ("Not me gatekeeping my dog's joint secret 😌"). Wide net, kill losers, scale winners.
  • 🎬Format consistency, tone variety: all video, but tone spans Gen-Z casual through clinical authority. Different tones fit different audience segments inside the same flagship offer.

🔑 What to Steal

  1. 1Pixel-seed the retargeting audience before you try to close. Run no-CTA awareness videos to build a warm custom audience, then close that warm pool with the direct offer. Two-step beats one-step on cold CPM.
  2. 2Frame the competitor (the incumbent solution) as the problem. Pawfy doesn't position against other supplements — they position against vets. For categories where the incumbent is frustrating (vets, prescription drugs, expensive treatments), name them and reframe them as inadequate.
  3. 3Pick one mechanism and hammer it. "Gut houses 80% of immune system." Mechanism repetition across creatives is what builds brand-level positioning, not category-level positioning.
  4. 4Scale the winner aggressively. When one hook works ("Over 20 million dogs…"), don't test new hooks — run 9 near-identical versions of the winner simultaneously. Variance comes from creative cuts, not copy rewrites.
  5. 5One long-form advertorial does the heavy belief work. "The Easy Mobility Method" page is vet-reviewed, fake-scarcity-anchored, with a 4-stage outcome timeline. The product page only has to convert intent — the advertorial converts skepticism.
  6. 6Parallel funnels per SKU, all spend on one flagship. 5 SKUs each have a funnel, but Hip & Joint takes ~85% of the ad volume. Spread coverage, concentrate spend.
  7. 7Cast a wide creative net for tone, narrow for format. Stat hook, curiosity, story, empathy, fear, problem-aware, pattern-interrupt — Pawfy tests every angle but stays 100% video. Find your winning tone; lock the format.
  8. 8Cross-source your competitor research. Meta Ad Library alone misses ads that have rotated out. Pair with a historical-corpus tool (Foreplay, spy.mgoffredi.com). Single source = blind spots.
