🔑 What to Steal

  1. 1Open with the exact micro-moment the audience already recognizes. "Grunting when you get off the couch" is so specific that anyone over 45 sees themselves in it instantly. Avoid abstract pain frames ("low energy", "mobility decline") in favor of the small embarrassing detail.
  2. 2Lock the copy, vary the page running it. Same primary-text block runs from Movesmethod (main), vanja.moves, and Josh Fear. Three pages, one validated copy block, three audience-identification signals to Meta's algorithm.
  3. 3Creator-page co-branding ("vanja.moves with Movesmethod"). The page name itself encodes the partnership. Reads as creator endorsement to the user; reads as a separate ad-account on the Meta side.
  4. 4Run 5 landing-page variants on one domain, concentrate volume on the winner. One URL (/toolkit-7479) takes the bulk of spend; the rest are versioned tests. Don't pick a winner blind; let the data on volume tell you.
  5. 5Verify format claims with Meta's own filter. DOM extraction can misclassify (video posters look like image tags). Use media_type=image / media_type=video filters when stating "100%" anything.
  6. 6Reverse-search the landing domain. The main brand's page shows 23 ads. Reverse-search by movesmethod.com surfaces ~17 more ads from creator pages — a third of the total volume that single-page tracking misses.
