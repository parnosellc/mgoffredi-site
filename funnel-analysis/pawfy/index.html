The Strategy

🎯 The Offer & Positioning

  • 💊Product: Pawfy Allergy & Immune — a dog supplement framed around upgrading quality of life by relieving allergies and constant itching.
  • 🐕Target buyer: Dog owners watching their dog suffer with allergies / scratching / itchy skin. High emotional load, willingness to pay for relief.
  • 🪝The hook: Emotional opener (“does your dog suffer from…”) plus the implicit promise that this is the supplement version of a vet visit.
  • 🌍Geo: UK-led (pawfy.co.uk apex domain, “Allergy & Immune” on-page) with US presence via pawfy.com for discount/retargeting links.

🔀 The Funnel Architecture

  • 📖Funnel A — Advertorial pre-sell (7 ads). Ads point to campaigns.pawfy.com/advertorial-article-version-aic-desktop-3. An editorial-style article that builds belief in the product before the buy decision — the classic supplement pre-sell move for cold traffic.
  • 🛍Funnel B — Direct product page (9 ads). Ads point straight to pawfy.co.uk/products/allergy-immune. Warmer audience or higher-intent placements where the advertorial step is skippable.
  • 🔗Plus a small retargeting / social layer: a discount-coded URL (pawfy.com/discount/CMCFB20) and an instagram.com/pawfy link that lift cross-platform pixel pools.

📝 The Copy Pattern

  • ✨Emotional opener. “Upgrade your dog’s quality of life” / “Does your dog suffer from allergies and constant itching?” — invokes the pain the owner sees daily.
  • 🔥Benefit-stacked body. Same formula as other supplement brands — the relief promise plus ingredient/mechanism credibility.
  • 🎛️Single-page operation, no avatar trinity. Unlike multi-page brands, Pawfy ran everything from one FB page. No doctor avatars, no creator partnerships visible in this snapshot. The brand persona is Pawfy.

⁉️ Why they paused (speculation)

  • 📉Pet-supplement is a tough category. Meta has historically restricted health claims for supplements, especially around “cures” for itching/allergy. Possible the account got flagged.
  • 🏗️Rebrand or restructure. The brand might be retooling — new creative cycle, new agency, repositioning. The advertorial URL slug (aic-desktop-3) shows they were already iterating creative.
  • 💸Cash / margin issue. Common in DTC supplements that scale fast then stall on payback.

All three are guesses; the public Ad Library doesn’t tell you the reason. Worth checking their shop, social, and reviews to confirm whether the brand is still operating.

🔑 What to Steal (from the historical run)

  1. 1Run an advertorial layer in front of the product page. For pet wellness or supplement categories specifically, the advertorial pre-sell does the educational lift on cold traffic so the product page only has to do the close.
  2. 2Two parallel funnels by audience temperature. Cold traffic → advertorial. Warmer traffic / re-engagement → direct product page. Pawfy split the volume roughly 50/50 between the two.
  3. 3Geo with separate domains, not a country selector. pawfy.co.uk for the UK market, pawfy.com for US. Two TLDs = two sets of pricing, currency, and compliance, without a clunky country picker on the homepage.
  4. 4Emotional opener for pet-pain advertising. The owner watches the dog suffer — lead the ad with the pain they see daily, not with the ingredient list.
  5. 5Watch competitor pauses, not just competitor activity. A brand going dark can mean a category opportunity — someone’s share of voice just opened up. Worth checking on Pawfy quarterly to see if they relaunch.
