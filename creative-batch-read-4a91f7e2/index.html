The 20-second version

The two ranges are at very different stages.

8518–8537 is SHA004 “Concept 1” — a video series that started decent, then faded at scale and has been killed. It opened at a $97 CPA in week one; as it scaled, CPA climbed to a $182 final week (~61% above the Toolkit average). Spend was throttled down fast as it slipped, then cut to zero on Jul 30. Almost all of it rode on one hook that burned out.

8797–8827 is HAR001 — a batch of static image ads. Published to Facebook on Jul 28 (real FB ad IDs) but with the ads switched off — in queue to be tested. Zero impressions and zero spend. There's no performance to report; they simply haven't run yet.

✕ The verdict: a loser at scale — wound down cleanly

Lifetime CPA was $137, but in its final week it ran $182 — about 61% above the $113 Toolkit average, among the worst of the meaningful Toolkit series (SHA005 held $123, JOR005 $119).

Spend was pulled back fast the moment it slipped — from a ~$7.2K peak week (Jul 13–19) to ~$640 the final week, then cut to $0 on Jul 30. A controlled wind-down, not a runaway.
