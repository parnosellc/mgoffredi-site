The building blocks behind the ads.

Scoring hooks, bodies and CTAs on their own — pooled across every creative that used them — surfaces a few clear signals. (Full detail in the Segment Analysis report.)

One set — hook SL0297 + body SM0038 + CTA SC0044 — is the best hook (60% hook rate), best body and best CTA all at once: ~1,400 sales at A$94. Nearly all of FCVSL's success traces to it — powerful, but a concentration risk.

No single bad piece — the whole TK component set runs hot. Even the best TK body & CTA sit at ~A$113, all over the A$96 target. It needs fresh building blocks, not a tweak.

🩸 Cut the bleeders

A few widely-used segments quietly waste spend: FCVSL bodies SM0014 (A$308 · 20 ads) and SM0015 (A$279 · 16), and TK CTA SC0050 (A$184 · 24 ads — the most-used TK CTA).

⬆️ Use more

Your best TK pieces — body SM0037 and CTA SC0043 (~A$113) — appear in just 2 creatives each. Prime candidates to build the fresh TK creative around.

05 · State of Toolkit (TK)

Pivoting strategy — both media buying and creative.

TK is steady and profitable. The setup I built prioritized stability — and it delivered. The proven creatives have since burned out, and it's time to move to the next phase: a deliberate shift on both the media-buying and creative sides.

Over the last 30 days I ran 167 new TK videos through testing. Only a handful cleared the bar — 102 flopped and just one scaled into a real winner. It's a serious challenge, and a clear signal that the current setup has hit its ceiling: it protected performance beautifully for months, but it's no longer surfacing enough new winners on its own. Time to adjust.

🔀 The setup that worked — and why it's evolving

The setup I built for TK prioritized stability — keep the budget on proven winners and protect performance while VSL was the priority. It was the right call and it delivered for months. Two things have shifted since: those proven creatives have burned out (natural fatigue), and I'm now pushing new creative harder — which the shared old-and-new campaign structure makes hard to read cleanly. So it's time for the next phase: fresh creative, on a clean dedicated testing setup.

VSL never needed this — a newer offer with no back-catalog to lean on, so its testing was clean by default. For TK, that back-catalog was exactly the stability lever I wanted — and now it's time to refresh it.

🎯Media-buying pivot

Going forward: a completely separate testing campaign for TK — no old creatives mixed in. New creative gets its own clean, dedicated budget and honest, comparable reads. Fewer tests at a time, each with enough spend to actually prove a winner.

🎨Creative pivot — ask to the team

Shift toward lander-specific TK creatives, built to match the new lander tests (the disfunction / bali / squat congruent-lander approach) rather than generic one-size-fits-all video. Ad + lander congruence is already showing promise — let's build for it deliberately.
