📌 What's the difference?
Nikkud (ניקוד) = vowel points in Hebrew text. The versions with nikkud have more accurate pronunciation from the AI because the vowels are explicitly marked. Recommended for learners!

Teaching Method

  • Mnemonic stories - Vivid visual associations for each word
  • Active recall pauses - 3-second pauses to answer before reveals
  • Spaced repetition - Review after every few words + cumulative final review
  • Chunking - Small groups of 2-4 words, then build up
